# Poster Layout

- **Canvas:** 48 x 36 inch landscape; white field, navy title/footer bands,
  restrained blue accent.
- **Header:** title and subtitle on a navy band; no author names are invented.
- **Upper left:** motivation and system architecture.
- **Upper middle:** three-stage SDR -> dual-DC recovery -> fixed-assignment
  certification, with dual-DC ablation below.
- **Upper right:** M9 method comparison and high-dimensional M12 sweep.
- **Bottom strip:** CSI robustness, pressure geometry stress test, and a large
  takeaway panel.
- **Footer:** reporting convention plus editable author/affiliation/QR area.

### Figure priority

1. Architecture diagram establishes the modelling distinction.
2. Method-comparison chart is the primary empirical result.
3. Dual-DC ablation explains why the proposed recovery is necessary.
4. CSI, pressure geometry, and M12 sweep establish robustness and scale.

### Print guidance

Use the PPTX for editing and export at the venue's required dimensions.  Before
printing, replace the editable footer text with authors, affiliations, contact
details, and an optional QR code; verify the venue's bleed and color-profile
requirements.

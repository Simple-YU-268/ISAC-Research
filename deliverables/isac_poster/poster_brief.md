# Poster Brief

## Audience and goal

- **Audience:** communication, signal-processing, and ISAC researchers.
- **Format:** 48 x 36 inch landscape conference poster.
- **Goal:** explain why AP-target sensing association must be geometry-aware,
  and show that the proposed robust binary recovery produces physically
  feasible, power-efficient covariance designs.

## Core takeaway

Dedicated sensing waveforms should be sparsely authorized using AP-target FIM
geometry, while communication beams remain globally cooperative.  The dual-DC
recovery plus fixed-assignment validation converts this principle into
physically feasible binary sensing clusters.

## Content priorities

1. Architecture distinction between globally cooperative communication and
   target-specific sensing authorization.
2. Three-stage optimization/recovery procedure.
3. Dual-DC ablation, 30-seed method comparison, CSI robustness, pressure
   geometries, and M12 scalability.
4. Tight PCRB tracking and explicit feasibility-first reporting convention.

- The paper source leaves author and affiliation fields blank; the poster
  deliberately leaves the footer as an editable author/affiliation/QR area.
- No conference or institutional logo was supplied.
- All quantitative statements are taken from the audited numerical results.

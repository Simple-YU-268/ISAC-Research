\begin{abstract}
This thesis studies robust power-minimizing resource allocation for a
cell-free massive MIMO integrated sensing and communication network. Distributed
access points cooperatively serve communication users and transmit
target-specific sensing waveforms under norm-bounded channel-state uncertainty.
The proposed formulation jointly guarantees worst-case communication SINR,
sensing SINR, and a matrix PCRB constraint while selecting a fixed-size sensing
cluster for each active target. In contrast to formulations that credit all
transmit covariance to sensing, the tracking FIM here is generated only by the
known waveform dedicated to the corresponding target. The resulting
mixed-integer non-convex problem is transformed through covariance lifting, an
S-Procedure LMI, an exact Schur-complement PCRB LMI, and joint rank-one/binary
DC penalties. A topology-aware SCA recovery procedure finally re-solves fixed
binary candidates and validates the extracted physical beamformers.
\end{abstract}
